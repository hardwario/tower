# TOWER hardware test run 2026-11-09: results

The two-board verification of the 2026-10-17 batch (TASKS.md, T9), re-run in full on the taf
firmware: since 2026-10-18 the node side is taf (`docs/firmware/taf.md`), with the power-up
gesture for pairing and factory reset (T20) and the `Info` retry (T19).

Boards: the HIL [core] Core Module r2 (`hio-core-module-2416969388`, `/dev/cu.usbserial-1140`) with
`radio_button`, its EEPROM wiped by the factory-reset gesture first; the Radio Dongle
(`/dev/cu.usbserial-DO00DEFS`) with `radio_gateway`; two more nodes paired throughout
(`radio_control` on an r2, `radio_thermostat` on the r1). No J-Link, no PPK2. Host: `tower`
from the same commit, `tower gateway --service` with its embedded broker. The node's gesture was
driven with `button simulate <ms>` sent within 3 s of its boot flash.

## Node kinds across a bridge restart (T23)

The kind byte in the gateway's registry (D5 (a)). The registry's record layout changed
(`FORMAT` 2), so the dongle's stored registry was moved across it, the way a replacement dongle
takes over (docs/cli/cli.md, *Moving a node key*). Boards: the Radio Dongle with
`radio_gateway`; nodes `control` (`radio_control`, listening, on the strip board), `r2`
(`radio_thermostat`, sleeping, LCD Module) and `btn-r1` (the r1, unplugged throughout). The nodes
kept their firmware: the radio tag did not move.

| Item | Verdict | Evidence |
|------|---------|----------|
| Key move across the format change | PASS | `tower nodes show <node> --keys --json` for all three with the old `tower` and bridge, the bridge stopped, the new CLI installed, the dongle flashed and wiped (`system eeprom wipe confirm`; its settings, address included, unchanged), the new bridge, `node_add` per node with `tx_floor` + 4096 and `rx_floor`: all three `ok`, kinds `?` |
| The kind stored from a `NodeInfo` | PASS | `r2` reported 2 min after the re-add and `control` 4 min after; both showed their kind |
| A bridge restart | PASS | 1 s after the restart `tower nodes list` showed `control` and `thermostat`, read from the registry (no `NodeInfo` this run); `set/brightness 15` (control) and `set/hysteresis 0.3` (r2) a second later were both queued (`set ... queued for node ...`), none refused; control's set delivered at once (`tx c58d0464 delivered`) and confirmed by its report (`state/brightness` 15), which also shows the node accepting the gateway's raised TX counter; r2's waited on the gateway and went out with its next heartbeat 11 min later (`tx df438142 delivered`), confirmed by its report (`state/hysteresis` 0.3) |
