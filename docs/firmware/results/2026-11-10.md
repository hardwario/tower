# TOWER hardware test run 2026-11-10: results

The Climate, CO2 and Relay Modules (TASKS.md, T5, T29, T6; `docs/firmware/modules.md`), and what
the night's other work could be checked with on the same bench.

Boards: a Core Module r2 (`hio-core-module-2416969388`, the HIL [core] slot) carrying a Climate
Module (first revision: SHT20, OPT3001, MPL3115A2, TMP112), a CO2 Module and a Relay Module, all
three on I2C; a Core Module r1 with no module. No Radio Dongle, no PPK2, no J-Link, nobody at the
bench. For the radio the r1 ran `radio_gateway` (feature `core-module-r1`) under `tower gateway
--service` with its embedded broker; the r2 was provisioned to it over its cable. Host: `tower`
built from the tree under test (radio tag 0x4cf2).

## The bridge: sets beyond the gateway's queue (T30)

Found while setting the climate node's seven settings in one go: the gateway holds two commands
per node, and the bridge dropped every further `set` without a word (it had logged each as
queued; the gateway's refusal was not logged). Two of seven reached the node.

| Item | Verdict | Evidence |
|------|---------|----------|
| Before | FAIL | seven sets published, seven logged `queued`, two delivered, `tower nodes list` back at 0 pending, five gone |
| After (the sets wait in the bridge) | PASS | seven sets published: two queued, five logged `waits for node ...: its queue on the gateway is full`, 7 pending in `tower nodes list`; one click on the node and all seven were delivered within 8 s (seven `tx delivered`), the node's `system settings print` showing all seven values |

## A listening node on US 915 (T3)

`radio_control` on the r2, `radio_gateway` on the r1, both on `us915`; sets published to MQTT
by a script that waits for the confirming `state` report and then 0.1 to 1 s (the strip task
rendering throughout, no strip attached).

| Item | Verdict | Evidence |
|------|---------|----------|
| Cable provisioning, the schedule found | PASS | provisioned over the cable; the node's `Info` and the time sync 46 s later; `node: hop schedule locked` 40 s after a boot |
| A `set`, first look | PASS | 12 of 12 confirmed in 457 to 690 ms (median 584 ms; the budget is 2.5 s) |
| 200 sets, as merged | FAIL | 189 of 200 confirmed, median 626 ms, with four stalls of 12 to 50 s. Again with the node's log: 84 of 90, two stalls, each `report uplink not delivered (no ACK)`, then `hop schedule lost, scanning` 4 s later and `hop schedule locked` 31 to 37 s after that. Idle for four minutes the node kept the schedule |
| The cause | found | every beacon was missed from one moment on (8 misses fail the uplink, 24 drop the anchor). A beacon's receive time is stamped when the task runs; under an EEPROM write (one follows every downlink) it is tens of ms late, and the slot clock anchored on it opens every later window after its beacon began. The gateway kept running (no gap in its stats stream) |
| 200 sets, with the fix | PASS | `tracked_boundary_ms`: 200 of 200 confirmed, min 447 ms, median 627 ms, p90 737 ms, the slowest 1385 ms, none over 2.5 s; no `hop schedule lost` in the node's log |
