### Root cause (gateway log + MQTT timeline)

1. **Lost downlink ACKs.** 44 of 118 shell downlinks were reported unconfirmed (`tx ... pending`;
   A 32 of 69, B 12 of 49), although the node ran the command. In a dual dialog the other node's
   uplink, 23 to 27 dB stronger, lands on the node's ACK of the downlink.
2. **Retry storm.** The gateway keeps an unconfirmed item and sends it again after *every* later
   uplink of that node, the reply's own chunks included (the node does not listen mid-reply), each
   attempt `DOWNLINK_REPS` = 2 confirmed sends, ~0.5 s out of receive. The other node's click and
   the replying node's next chunk go unheard behind these (B's lost clicks, A's cut replies).
3. **Empty success.** When the first run's reply was lost altogether, the re-sent command meets the
   node's dedup ("repeated, not run again"), which answers with an empty `last` frame at chunk 0:
   the host takes it for a complete, empty reply and exits 0 (the 4 empty replies; e.g. minute 45:
   A ran the command at 01:24:34, its chunk 0 never arrived, the re-sent command at 01:26:01 was
   answered empty).
4. **Same pattern, both clicks at once** (the scratch harness, 11 trials before it was stopped): A
   failed 3 times, its click logged `uplink not delivered (no ACK)` although the gateway received
   it (and published it): the gateway's ACK to A was lost under B's chunk, the gateway then spent
   its downlink attempt on A (not listening), and A's retransmits went unheard. A retransmit the
   gateway does hear is re-ACKed with the pending flag, but no downlink follows it (only a fresh
   uplink reaches `on_uplink`), so the node's window opens for nothing.

### Fixes

- `cli/src/gateway/engine.rs`: a reply chunk proves the command delivered; if the gateway has not
  reported it delivered, the host drops it from the gateway's queue (`QueueDrop`, best effort):
  no more re-sends after each chunk. Two engine tests.
- `firmware/apps/radio_push_button.rs`: the dedup remembers whether the first run's reply all went
  up; a repeat of one that did not answers with a chunk gap (the host reports the reply truncated,
  exit 1) and the text `(reply lost on the air: ran once, not run again)`, never an empty success.
- Tried and reverted (no measurable effect in the A/B below): `CHUNK_REPS` 5 to `MAX_REPS` (10),
  so a chunk outlasts a busy gateway.
- Tried and reverted (never triggered in the 70 trials it ran in, so unexercised on hardware; item 4
  stays open): `recv` recording a retransmit it re-ACKed with the pending flag
  (`Net::take_pending_reack`) and the gateway then delivering the queued downlink. It would be
  bounded like the re-ACK itself (`MAX_REACKS` per counter), but a replayed capture of a node's
  last frame could then also make the gateway try a queued downlink (sealed for the node,
  airtime only).
- Not fixed (design): the gateway's first downlink attempt goes out whether or not the node got
  the ACK (~0.5 s out of receive when it did not), and one streaming dialog takes airtime from
  the other nodes (no pacing between chunks; on US 915 the slots pace it).

## A/B: two remote shells at once, committed vs the fixes

Each arm on a fresh EU 868 product (all three boards reflashed, EEPROMs wiped, both nodes
cable-paired), 40 trials in the soak's timing: both shells queued (`--timeout 60000`), 2 s later a
click on A, then on B ~0.13 s after; whatever a failed trial left queued on the gateway dropped
before the next trial, so trials are independent. A trial counts as ok only with exit 0 and the
full reply.

| Arm | A (r2, -50 dBm) | B (rev 1.3, -23 dBm) | Clicks on MQTT | Downlinks unconfirmed | Leftovers |
|-----|-----------------|----------------------|----------------|-----------------------|-----------|
| committed (5d08387 images, installed `tower`) | 28 of 40 | 36 of 40 | 79 of 80 | 48 (69 confirmed) | 11 |
| all four fixes (`CHUNK_REPS` 10) | **38 of 40** | 32 of 40 | 77 of 80 | 77 (36 confirmed) | 10 |
| the fixes with `CHUNK_REPS` 5 (stopped at 30) | 12 of 30 | 25 of 30 | | 54 (23 confirmed) | |

These arms are **not comparable**: A's rate swings from 28 to 38 of 40 to 12 of 30 between
configurations whose differences cannot explain it (in the third arm A mostly never received the
command: no chunk at all, the downlink unconfirmed after its click). The confounder is the click
timing: the second click follows the first `tower exec` by its run time (~0.13 s, host-load
dependent), and that offset decides which frames collide (A's downlink ACK, B's click, the
gateway's second downlink attempt). What the arms do show regardless of it: with the host's
`QueueDrop` the per-chunk re-sends are gone (`shell chunk for unknown cmd_id` 10 to 0), and the
re-ACK delivery never triggered in 70 trials (unexercised on hardware). Re-run with the click
order and the gap randomized below.

### Randomized click timing (the fair comparison)

The same procedure, 30 trials per arm, each trial clicking A or B first (random) and the other
0 to 0.6 s after the first `tower exec` returns (random).

| Arm | A | B | Clicks on MQTT | Downlinks unconfirmed | Failed trials, clicks < 0.1 s apart | Failed trials, clicks >= 0.1 s apart |
|-----|---|---|----------------|-----------------------|------------------------------------|--------------------------------------|
| committed | 27 of 30 | 28 of 30 | 60 of 60 | 4 (56 confirmed) | 3 of 7 | 0 of 23 |
| all four fixes | 29 of 30 | 29 of 30 | 60 of 60 | 3 (58 confirmed) | 1 of 4 | 1 of 26 |
| the fixes + a 150 ms gap between chunks (scratch build, not in the tree) | 28 of 30 | 28 of 30 | 59 of 60 | 33 (43 confirmed) | 0 of 2 | 4 of 28 |

Conclusions:
- Dual dialogs fail almost only when the two nodes' clicks fall within ~0.1 s (plus the ~0.13 s
  `tower exec` run time) of each other: the collision window. The earlier fixed-offset arms put
  every trial inside it, which is why they failed so often and so erratically.
- In the window the fixes are not measurably better at this sample size (1 of 4 vs 3 of 7). What
  they verifiably change: no per-chunk re-sends after a lost downlink ACK (the soak's retry
  storm), and no empty success (the soak's 4); `CHUNK_REPS` 10 cannot be shown to help or hurt.
- Pacing the chunks is worse (8x the unconfirmed downlinks, failures outside the window): not
  adopted.
- The remaining failures in the window are airtime contention that none of the fixes address:
  while the gateway delivers one node's downlink (up to ~0.5 s, deaf to everything else) the other
  node's click goes unACKed, and its retransmits, stronger at the first node than the gateway is,
  cover that node's downlink or its ACK.

The bench was left on the images of the four-fix tree (both push buttons and the Dongle gateway),
EU 868, both nodes paired; the gateway service stopped. Committed: the host's `QueueDrop` and the
node's lost-reply dedup; `CHUNK_REPS` and the re-ACK delivery were reverted.
